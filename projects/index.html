projects

Discovering Hidden Structure

Finding the sparse rules that generate complex behavior, from data.

Equation Discovery (SINDy)

Data-driven identification of governing equations using sparse regression. Given time-series measurements of a dynamical system, recover the equations that generated them. Sparse, interpretable, and physically meaningful.

sparse regression dynamical systems

Deep Delay Autoencoders

Combining Takens' delay embedding theorem with deep autoencoders to discover latent coordinates from partial observations. Recovering full-state dynamics from a single measured variable.

autoencoders delay embedding

Dimensionally Consistent Learning

Integrating physical units directly into ML architectures. Algorithms that automatically discover dimensionless groups via the Buckingham Pi theorem, improving generalization by respecting the structure of physical law.

dimensional analysis physics-informed ML

Bridging Scales

How structure at one level gives rise to behavior at another.

Multiscale Materials Modeling

Stochastic modeling of granular materials under extreme conditions. Understanding how microstructural heterogeneity drives macroscopic phenomena like hotspot formation and reaction initiation.

stochastic PDEs materials science

Arrow of Time

A theoretical paper arguing that time's arrow emerges from the act of observation itself. Connecting Feynman-Kac path integrals, coarse-graining, and the Mori-Zwanzig formalism to show how irreversibility arises from projection.

statistical mechanics philosophy of physics

R(D)-SINDy Autoencoder

Rate-distortion theory meets equation discovery. An autoencoder that jointly learns a compressed representation and sparse governing equations, balancing reconstruction fidelity against model complexity.

information theory scientific ML
