View

Reading the rope. Amber is taut, gray-blue is slack. Whips are tension collapse: watch the color die just before the crack. Weight enters through drag: a heavy rope is a low drag setting, a light rope is a high one. Stiffness sweeps chain to rod. Mode buttons set the drive to the hanging-chain resonances fₙ = j₀ₙ√(g/L)/4π.
