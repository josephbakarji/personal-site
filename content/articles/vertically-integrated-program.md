# Vertically Integrated Project Program (VIPP) in Music Technology

## Overview

The Music Intelligence Lab now offers a Vertically Integrated Project Program (VIPP). Through this initiative, students can earn course credit, participate in long-term research, and explore music and technology intersections. Projects are team-based, interdisciplinary, and evolve across semesters with undergraduate, graduate, and faculty collaboration.

## 1. Music in Motion (Physical Intelligence)

Sound emerges from "motion, vibration, and embodied performance." This thrust encompasses:

* Instrument design: creating new physical and digital interfaces for music-making
* Acoustics and physics of sound: studying oscillations, resonance, and timbre
* Gesture-to-sound mapping: converting movement into expressive sound

Current focus areas include developing a smart music glove instrument (a wearable interface translating hand gestures into sound) and mapping gestures to sound in Virtual Reality environments.

## 2. Mathematics of Music (Symbolic Intelligence)

Music contains "hidden structures" (scales, rhythms, transitions) that can be revealed through theory and mathematics. This thrust explores:

* Music information retrieval (MIR): extracting structure from audio
* Graph and network modeling: representing musical systems as interconnected structures
* Dynamical systems: viewing music as evolving trajectories in time and space

Current projects include revealing graph-based structure in Arabic Maqamat, mapping modal systems onto analyzable networks for music generation, and exploring symbolic music representations from audio recordings using machine learning.

## 3. Generative Music & AI (Machine Intelligence)

With AI and machine learning advances, the lab explores music generation, improvisation, and human-machine collaboration:

* Generative models: creating new music using AI
* Human-AI co-creation: maintaining performer involvement
* Interpretable systems: ensuring AI-generated music reflects meaningful structure

The lab aims to develop "AI-driven improvisation systems inspired by Arabic music traditions," integrating generative models with custom interfaces.

## Join the Program

The VIPP welcomes undergraduate, master's, and PhD students. Responsibilities vary by level: prototyping instruments, coding algorithms, or developing music intelligence theories. Projects are hands-on, collaborative, and creative.
